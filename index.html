About

I'm in the third year of a Computer Science (Hons) degree at IIMS, Kathmandu, with my electives and spare hours pointed squarely at Artificial Intelligence. Most of what I know came from building: I'd rather have a rough system running end-to-end than a perfect diagram of one.

That habit shows up in my work. For the Fusemachines AI Fellowship I built a legal-aid assistant that refuses to answer outside its ten ingested statutes. For my capstone I merged ten-plus public datasets and benchmarked four CNN architectures before picking one. For my own curiosity I wrote a multi-agent coding assistant that won't touch a file until you approve its plan.

What I'm after next is a team where I can ship real systems, get my code reviewed by people better than me, and keep compounding.
